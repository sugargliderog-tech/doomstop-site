How do I protect apps?

Open DOOMSTOP → Settings → Doom Protection. Allow Screen Time access, choose your distracting apps in Apple’s picker, then turn protection on. Doom Protection needs iOS 16.4 or later; asking “why?” right on the shield needs iOS 26.4, opening DOOMSTOP from the shield needs iOS 26.5.

I really need an app right now

On the shield, tap “I have a reason…” and pick one – the app unlocks for 10 minutes of use and locks again by itself. You can also turn Doom Protection off anytime.

How do I turn everything off?

  • DOOMSTOP → Settings → Doom Protection → Disable protection
  • iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → DOOMSTOP (revokes access completely)
  • Doomling interventions: DOOMSTOP → Settings, or iOS Settings → Notifications → DOOMSTOP

Does DOOMSTOP know which apps I use?

No. Apple only gives DOOMSTOP anonymous tokens and “has passed 15/30/45… minutes today” signals. See the privacy policy.

I deleted the app and my Doomling is gone

DOOMSTOP keeps everything on your iPhone only – no account, no cloud. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Sorry, little guy.
