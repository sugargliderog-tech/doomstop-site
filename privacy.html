DOOMSTOP

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

The short version: DOOMSTOP has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone. We – the developer – never receive any of your data.

Who we are

DOOMSTOP is made by Julian Tscharner, an independent developer (“we”). Contact: sugargliderog@gmail.com.

What DOOMSTOP stores – only on your device

  • Your game progress: XP, levels, DoomCoins, quests, chores, your Doomling, cosmetics, achievements, settings and the history of your “HOLD UP” choices. Stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone.
  • Proof photos (optional): only if you choose to add a photo when finishing a quest. Copied into the app’s private folder on your iPhone. Never uploaded.
  • Screen Time information (optional, see below): stored in a private area shared only between DOOMSTOP and its own Screen Time extensions on your iPhone.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. DOOMSTOP does not make network requests to any server operated by us or by third parties.

Screen Time (Doom Protection)

If you turn on Doom Protection, DOOMSTOP uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) with your permission. Apple designed these to protect your privacy, and DOOMSTOP works within them:

  • You choose apps and websites in Apple’s own picker. DOOMSTOP only receives opaque tokens from Apple – it cannot see which apps or websites you picked, and it cannot read their names.
  • DOOMSTOP never sees what you do inside other apps.
  • Apple only tells DOOMSTOP when your selected apps pass certain amounts of use in a day (for example “at least 30 minutes”). DOOMSTOP keeps these steps on your iPhone to show your Doomling’s mood and to give small rewards. It never receives exact usage times.
  • Screen Time information is never transmitted, sold, shared or used for advertising.

You can revoke Screen Time access anytime in iOS Settings → Screen Time, or turn Doom Protection off in DOOMSTOP.

Notifications

If you switch on “Doomling interventions”, your iPhone shows occasional local notifications. They are created on your device – there is no push server. They never contain app names or usage times.

Camera and photo library

Only used when you actively choose to take or pick a proof photo for a finished quest. The photo stays on your iPhone.

Sharing

When you tap “Share my DOOMSTOP”, the app creates an image of your Doomling and a few stats and opens the iOS Share Sheet. Nothing is sent until you pick a destination yourself (for example Messages or Instagram) – and then that service’s own privacy policy applies. The share card never contains app names, Screen Time details, quest texts, photos or your name.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising and no data brokers
  • No tracking across apps or websites (App Tracking Transparency is not needed because nothing is tracked)

Apple

DOOMSTOP is distributed through the App Store. Apple may process data according to its own privacy policy (for example for downloads, Screen Time and crash reports you choose to share with developers). We have no access to this data beyond Apple’s anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → “Reset all progress” in DOOMSTOP, or delete the app – this removes everything DOOMSTOP stored on your iPhone. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored with us to delete.

Children

DOOMSTOP is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone – it does not collect personal data at all.

Your rights

Under data protection laws such as the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), you have rights regarding your personal data. Since DOOMSTOP keeps all data on your device and we hold none, you control it fully yourself. For any question, contact sugargliderog@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If DOOMSTOP ever starts sending data anywhere, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that happens.
